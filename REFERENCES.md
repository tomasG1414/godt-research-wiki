# References

Every paper summarised in `wiki/sources/`, keyed by its page name (`[[key]]` in the wiki).
Links point to the publisher (DOI) or arXiv. **No full texts are distributed in this repository**;
obtain them from the publisher or the authors.

The source pages are summaries and commentary written with AI assistance and reviewed by the author. Short quotations are marked
with quotation marks and attributed to the paper they come from. They are used for
criticism and review (Berne Convention, Art. 10(1); Spain, Ley de Propiedad Intelectual, Art. 32).
Copyright in the quoted text stays with its authors and publishers. If you hold rights to any
quoted passage and want it removed, open an issue.
